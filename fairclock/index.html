How the score works

A meeting in someone's work hours costs them 0 points. Early or late (7am–10pm, outside work) costs 1. Night (10pm–7am) costs 3, because lost sleep hurts most.

How the rotation is chosen

Each week FairClock tries every half-hour slot and picks the one that keeps everyone's running total as even as possible. Whoever suffered last week gets the easy slot next.

Daylight saving included

Times are worked out for each real date, so clock changes in New York, London or Sydney are handled automatically.
