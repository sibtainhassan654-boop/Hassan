# Bill Banao

A free invoice maker for Pakistani freelancers. It runs entirely in the browser from one file: `index.html`.

- Bill clients in USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD or PKR
- Add Payoneer or bank (IBAN) payment details
- See the PKR equivalent at your own exchange rate
- Save as PDF, or copy as text for Fiverr, Upwork, email or WhatsApp
- Saves automatically in your browser. No sign-up, nothing uploaded

`revenue-lab.html` is an earlier page: a calculator for what a revenue goal takes.

## FairClock (`fairclock/index.html`)

Rotates a team's recurring meeting across time zones so the early mornings and late nights are shared evenly. Add people and their cities; it builds a 4–26 week schedule, compares it with "same time every week", and exports a calendar file (.ics) or a shareable team link. Runs entirely in the browser.
