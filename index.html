How to invoice a foreign client from Pakistan

1. Fill in both names

Your name or business name, and your client's company name exactly as they use it. Many companies only pay invoices that show their correct legal name.

2. Bill in the client's currency

A UK client expects GBP and a US client expects USD. It makes paying easy for them, and you get paid faster.

3. List the work clearly

Write one line per task, with quantity and rate. "Logo design, 3 concepts" is clearer than "design work" and leads to fewer questions.

4. Say how to pay

Add your Payoneer email or receiving account, or your bank name and IBAN. Double-check every digit before you send it.

5. Set a due date

"Net 15" means the client should pay within 15 days of the issue date. Agree on terms before you start the work.

6. Keep a record

Save every invoice as a PDF and keep it with the payment receipt. It helps with your bank, your taxes and any dispute.

Your invoices are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
