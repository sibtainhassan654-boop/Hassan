Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What you type stays on your device

Bill Banao runs entirely in your web browser. The names, addresses, amounts and payment details you enter are saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to us or to any server we control.

Clearing your data

To delete everything Bill Banao has saved, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Using the site in a private or incognito window means nothing is kept after you close it.

Fonts

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address with Google, as with any website that uses Google Fonts. See Google's privacy policy.

Advertising and analytics

Bill Banao does not currently show ads or use analytics. If that changes, this page will be updated first to explain what is collected and how to opt out.

No financial or tax advice

Bill Banao is a tool for making invoices. It does not give financial, legal or tax advice. Exchange rates you enter are your own and are used only for your reference.
